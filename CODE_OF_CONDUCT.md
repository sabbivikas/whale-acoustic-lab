# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, experiences, and scientific interpretations.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and repair harm.
- Put the community and responsible science ahead of individual promotion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, sexualized language or attention, threats, stalking, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, discriminatory comments, and publishing another person’s private information without permission are unacceptable. Misrepresenting uncertain scientific output as fact or knowingly publishing private recordings or annotations is also incompatible with this community.

## Enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public Issue, pull request, or
Discussion.

- While the repository is private, collaborators should open a draft GitHub
  Security Advisory and prefix the title with `Conduct report`.
- Before public participation begins, Vikas Sabbi must enable GitHub Private
  Vulnerability Reporting. Afterward, use
  `Security → Advisories → Report a vulnerability` and prefix the report title
  with `Conduct report`.

This repository channel keeps the report private without publishing a personal
email address. Reports are reviewed by the project maintainer, Vikas Sabbi.
The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful.
