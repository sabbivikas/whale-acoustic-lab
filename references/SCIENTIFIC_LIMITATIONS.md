# Scientific limitations

These files are verified examples from the public DSWP collection, not a labeled whale-language dictionary.

The public release provides no per-file rhythm type, click timestamps, social unit, individual whale identity, vowel or spectral class, recording date, exact location, or recording system. All such fields remain `null`. The dataset card says the recordings come from one vocal clan and geographic region, but it does not identify the clan; the manifest therefore does not assign a clan label.

WhAM embeddings measure similarity in a learned acoustic representation. They do not establish meaning, identity, social membership, rhythm type, or vowel class. A close embedding match must be described as acoustic/model-space similarity only. WhAM performs acoustic translation, not semantic translation of whale communication.

Dataset-level provenance—offshore Dominica, approximately 2,000 km², collected during 2005–2018—must not be presented as exact per-recording metadata.
